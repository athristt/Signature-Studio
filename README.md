## 📖 About

Signature Studio is a single page tool that creates a branded Skill Shikshya email signature. Type in your details, watch the signature update live, and copy it with one click. There is no build step, no dependencies and nothing to install.

## ✨ Features

- 👀 **Live preview** that updates as you type
- 🖼️ **Photo upload** with a circular crop, drag to reposition and slide to zoom
- ✏️ **Editable details**: full name, designation, phone and email
- 🎨 **Skill Shikshya branding** with the logo colours, social icons and website link built in
- 📋 **One click copy** that pastes cleanly into Gmail
- ↩️ **Revert to original** to reset everything

## 🚀 How to Access

**Option 1: Use it online**

Open **https://athristt.github.io/Signature-Studio/**

**Option 2: Run it on your computer**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/athristt/Signature-Studio.git
```

Then double click **`index.html`** and it opens in your browser.

## 📋 How to Use

1. Upload your photo and drag or zoom until it looks right
2. Fill in your name, designation, phone and email
3. Click **Copy signature**
4. In Gmail, go to **Settings → See all settings → General → Signature** and paste it there

> 💡 Paste into the Signature settings, not a new compose window, for the most reliable result. If your photo does not show after pasting, right click it in the preview to save it, then add it with Gmail's own image button.

## 🔒 Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. There is no backend and your details and photo are never uploaded anywhere.
